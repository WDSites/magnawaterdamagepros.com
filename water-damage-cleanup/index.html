Water Damage Cleanup In The First 48 Hours

Cleanup is urgent because wet materials start to smell, stain and break down within a day or two. Magna dispatch answers around the clock, and a crew can be sent the same day when one is available. In the meantime, stop the source if possible, keep pets and family out of dirty water and get anything valuable off the floor.

Open windows only if the weather allows, and leave the household vacuum out of it. Photograph the room first so your insurer can see its original condition.

What Cleanup Covers

Cleanup is the work of removing what the water ruined and cleaning what it touched. It follows the first stop-the-spread steps described under water mitigation and comes before rebuilding under water damage repair. The three together make up a full restoration.

In practice it means pulling wet carpet and pad, cutting out soaked drywall and insulation, hauling away debris, scrubbing hard surfaces with an antimicrobial cleaner and treating odors at the source.

When Standing Water Is An Emergency

Standing water that touches outlets, appliances or the panel is an electrical risk, so stay out of it. Call the utility or 911 for any electrical or gas concern before you call us. Sewage or river water brings contaminants into the home, and the area should be closed off until a crew has handled it.

Basement Water Cleanup

Basements collect water from window wells, floor drains, sump pump failures and foundation seepage. In Magna the usual triggers are spring runoff and heavy storms. The county's sandbags can steer water away from a door, but by the county's own account they will not seal it out, so a wet basement still needs extraction and cleanup.

Finished basements add complexity. Carpet, pad, baseboard and the bottom of the drywall come out first. Wood-framed walls are dried in place when possible, and the concrete slab is dried before any new flooring goes down.

Deodorizing And Sanitizing

Odor control follows removal and drying. Antimicrobial cleaners go on hard surfaces, and air scrubbers or foggers can treat what remains in the air. If a smell returns after a few days, it usually means a wet spot was missed, and the crew goes back with meters instead of adding more deodorizer.

Local Notes For Magna Owners

Older homes near Main Street often have crawl spaces or partial basements with dirt or thin concrete floors. Water that reaches them can sit for days without being seen, so check there after any leak. A sewer problem in the street belongs to the water district that serves your block, and the cleanup inside the house belongs to us.

Insurance Notes

Coverage for cleanup follows the cause. A sudden failure is commonly covered, while slow leaks and outside flooding often are not. Read your policy, and hold on to removed items until the adjuster has seen them, or photograph them first.

Cost Factors For Cleanup

  • Water category and whether sanitizing is needed.
  • Affected square footage, from one room to a whole floor.
  • Materials involved, such as pad, drywall or cabinetry.
  • Drying days and equipment if drying is part of the job.
  • Rebuild scope once the area is clean.
