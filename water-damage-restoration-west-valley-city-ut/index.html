The City And Its Water Problems

West Valley City had 140,230 residents at the 2020 census, which makes it the second-largest city in Utah. It was incorporated in 1980 by merging the communities of Granger, Hunter, Chesterfield and Redwood, and the housing varies a lot between them.

The east side, near Redwood and Chesterfield, is mostly brick ranch homes from the 1960s and 1970s. Their original water heaters, supply lines and shutoff valves are old enough to fail, so a slow leak behind a wall is common there. The west side, including West Hunter, Woodhaven and Oquirrh, is mostly 1990s and 2000s planned subdivisions where appliance hoses and ice maker lines are the usual culprits.

The Jordan River forms the city's eastern boundary and the Oquirrh Mountains rise to the west, so homes on the low east side deserve a check of the FEMA flood map at msc.fema.gov before anyone assumes the risk is small.
