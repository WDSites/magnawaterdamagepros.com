Who we are

How The Service Works

Magna Water Damage Pros answers calls for water damage and mold problems in Magna, UT, and sends licensed and insured restoration professionals to the job. Dispatch runs day and night because water does not keep business hours.

The business address is 8470 W 2700 S, Magna, UT 84044. Crews also work in West Valley City, Salt Lake City and West Jordan, and each of those towns has its own page with local notes.

What We Handle

The service list covers water mitigation, water damage cleanup, water damage repair, emergency water removal, flood damage restoration and mold remediation. The services page explains which one fits which situation.

How Pricing Is Handled

Scope and price are explained before work starts, and we post no flat prices because water damage varies too much for them to be accurate. The cost factors are water category, affected square footage, materials, drying days and rebuild scope, and each service page explains them.

What We Do Not Claim

This site carries no reviews, ratings or award badges, and it does not state licence numbers or certification counts. The published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage and S520 for mold guide our scopes, and any company, including this one, can be asked whether its crews follow them.

We do not promise insurance approval, rent out drying equipment, handle fire damage or do waterproofing.
