What To Have Ready

  • The address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water started and whether it has been stopped.
  • Which rooms are wet and roughly how deep the water is.
  • Whether it is clean water, wastewater or storm water.
  • Your insurer, if you plan to file a claim.

While You Wait

Close the main valve if a pipe is to blame. Switch off wet rooms at the breaker only from a dry spot, and phone the utility or 911 about electrical or gas danger. Photograph everything before you move it.
