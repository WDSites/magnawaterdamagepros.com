Flood Damage Restoration When Water Comes From Outside

Flood water enters a home from outside, through windows, doors, foundation walls or floor drains. If it is happening now in Magna, call the 24/7 dispatch line, stay out of the water and keep power off to wet areas only if the panel is reachable without stepping in it. Report any electrical or gas risk to the utility or 911 first.

Flood water is treated as contaminated until proven otherwise. Do not wade through it, do not let children or pets play in it, and wear boots and gloves if you must enter. A crew can often be sent the same day, and the process is the same after hours.

Flood Versus Other Water Damage

A burst pipe puts clean water into a home. A flood brings in whatever the ground, street or sewer was carrying, and that is why the cleaning rules are stricter. Under ANSI/IICRC S500, floodwater and sewage are Category 3, which means porous materials are removed and hard surfaces are cleaned and sanitized.

The difference also changes insurance. A sudden pipe break is often a homeowners claim, while floodwater falls under separate flood coverage, which the Utah Insurance Department says usually carries a waiting period of about 30 days.

Flood Risk Around Magna

Magna sits on the valley floor below the Oquirrh Mountains, south of the Great Salt Lake. Spring snowmelt and heavy storms raise creek and canal flows across Salt Lake County. Overland flooding along rivers, creeks and canals is Salt Lake County Flood Control's territory, and road flooding is handled by your local government.

Do not guess your flood zone, since three map tools show it by address: msc.fema.gov, floodhazards.utah.gov and the county floodplain map. Sandbags can be picked up at the county shed in Midvale, though they only redirect water.

Basement Flooding Cleanup

Basements go through a set order. Pumps clear deep water. Wet carpet, pad and the lower drywall are removed. The concrete slab and any remaining framing are dried with air movers and dehumidifiers, and surfaces are cleaned and sanitized if the water was gray or black.

If a basement floods again and again, restoration is not waterproofing. Ask a waterproofing or drainage contractor about the cause after the drying, and have the crew point out where water entered.

Sewage Backup Cleanup: What To Do First

Sewage backup is a health and safety hazard, so keep everyone out of the area and do not try to clean it yourself. Call the dispatch line, and if the problem is the street sewer, call your sewer provider. In much of Magna the sewer main belongs to Magna Water District, so a backup in the main may be theirs to clear.

A crew removes affected porous materials, bags and disposes of them, cleans and disinfects hard surfaces and dries the structure. Do not run washers, tubs or toilets until the line is cleared.

Insurance Notes

Look at your policy for flood coverage and its waiting period. The Utah Insurance Department's flood FAQ lists the claim line for NFIP policies, and privately written policies go through your agent. Photograph everything, and hold damaged items for the adjuster.
