What we do

Water Damage Restoration in Magna, UT

Water damage restoration is the work of removing water from a building, drying the materials that absorbed it, cleaning what the water touched and rebuilding what cannot be saved. In Magna, UT it usually starts with a phone call about a burst pipe, a failed water heater, a flooded basement or a ceiling that has started to drip.

Magna Water Damage Pros runs a 24/7 emergency dispatch line for Magna and nearby Salt Lake County towns. The first call covers three things: what the source is, whether it has been stopped, and which rooms are wet. From those answers the dispatcher can send the right crew and tell you what to do while you wait.

The published ANSI/IICRC S500 standard guides water damage work, and homeowners can ask any company whether its crews follow it. Moisture meters and thermal imaging show where water traveled behind walls. Air movers and dehumidifiers dry what can be saved, and anything that cannot be dried comes out.

Water categoryTypical sourceWhat it usually means
Category 1Supply line, sink overflow, rainwaterClean at the source and can turn worse if left wet for days
Category 2Washing machine or dishwasher dischargeContains contaminants, so porous materials are often removed
Category 3Sewage, river or surface floodwaterUnsafe, so crews contain the area and remove affected materials

The S500 standard sorts water by how contaminated it is. A crew confirms the category on site.

Local conditions

What Magna Homes Are Up Against

Magna sits south of the Great Salt Lake and below the Oquirrh Mountains, and the valley floor here is flat enough that water has nowhere to run once it gets inside. Local histories describe a wave of moderately priced single-family homes built south and east of Main Street in the 1970s. Houses of that age often still have original supply lines and water heaters, so a leak in one of them is worth taking seriously.

The older commercial blocks along Main Street, including the Empress Theatre, are brick buildings with roofs and plumbing that have been patched over many decades. If you manage a building like that, a roof leak that wets plaster or old flooring should be dried and checked quickly, because hidden moisture keeps working long after the stain stops spreading.

Spring is the other pressure point. During runoff season the county hands out sandbags from a shed at 604 West 6960 South in Midvale, and it cautions that sandbags will not seal out water. Use them to steer flow, then call for extraction if water still reaches a basement.

After the March 2020 earthquake centered in Magna, some owners also found new cracks and shifted fittings, so a plumber check of visible supply lines is a reasonable precaution.

We also take calls from properties near the Oquirrh foothills, along the Great Salt Lake shoreline and around Saltair. Homes on sloped lots should have the crew check where hillside runoff meets the foundation, since the wet spot inside is often only the end of a longer path.

What Sets The Cost Of A Water Loss

No two losses cost the same, so we do not post flat prices. These five factors decide most of the scope, and the dispatcher will ask about each one when you call.

Cost factorWhy it matters
Water categoryClean water is cheaper to handle than gray or sewage water, which needs containment and removal of porous materials
Affected square footageMore wet floor and wall area means more equipment and more drying time
Materials involvedHardwood, carpet pad, drywall and insulation each dry or fail differently
Drying days and equipmentAir movers and dehumidifiers run until moisture readings reach dry standards
Rebuild scopeReplacing drywall, flooring and cabinets is a separate step after drying
